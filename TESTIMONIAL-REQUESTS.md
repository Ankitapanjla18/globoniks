# Testimonial requests — ready to send

Three clients, three messages. Each asks for the one thing that makes a quote
worth publishing: **a concrete detail in their own words**, plus permission.
Send as WhatsApp or email; edit the tone to how you actually talk to them.

When a reply comes back, paste it verbatim into the project's `quote` field in
`src/data/projects.ts`:

```ts
quote: {
  text: "…their words, unpolished…",
  name: "Their name",
  role: "Their role",
  company: "Their company",
},
```

It then appears automatically on the case study and in the homepage
"In their words" section. Keep their permission reply on file.

---

## 1. Subhash Panjla — Tricity Life Insurance (named client, easiest ask)

> Subhash ji, quick request. We're putting client feedback on the Globoniks
> website and yours would mean a lot as our first named client.
>
> Could you send two or three lines, in your own words, on what the platform
> changed for you — for example how applicants reach you now compared to
> before? It doesn't need to be polished; honest is better.
>
> And a one-line "yes, you can publish this with my name and role" so we have
> your permission on record. Thank you!

**Publish as:** name "Subhash Panjla", role "LIC Career Mentor", company
"Tricity Life Insurance".

## Stronger than a quote, if the relationship allows

- A **LinkedIn recommendation** or post mentioning Globoniks — publicly
  verifiable; link it from the case study.
- A **Google review** on the Globoniks Business Profile (set the profile up
  first — it also feeds Mohali local search).
- A 30-second phone video saying the same thing.
